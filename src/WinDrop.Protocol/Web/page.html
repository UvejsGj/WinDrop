      Add WinDrop to the share sheet

      Make a Shortcut once, and Send to WinDrop appears when you tap Share in Photos or Files.

      1. Copy this link:
      2. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, and name the new shortcut Send to WinDrop.
      3. Open the shortcut's details and turn on Show in Share Sheet, accepting images, media and files.
      4. Add the action Get Contents of URL and paste the link as its URL. Show its options and set Method to POST and Request Body to Form.
      5. Add a field of type File, with the key file and the value Shortcut Input.
      6. In Photos, tap Share, then Send to WinDrop, and accept on the PC.

      It works while WinDrop is open on the PC and both are on the same Wi-Fi. If the PC's address changes, scan its code again and update the link.

      Files go straight to the PC over your Wi-Fi, and the PC asks before saving anything. Use this on a network you trust: the connection is not encrypted beyond the Wi-Fi itself.
